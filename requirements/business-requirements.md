# Business Requirements

## BR-01: Client monitoring value

The Investor Workspace should help a client monitor instruments of interest between visits without requiring the client to keep the product open continuously.

**Rationale:** Client Services feedback and product discovery interviews.

## BR-02: User control

A client should be able to choose which instruments and conditions to monitor and should be able to pause or remove an alert.

## BR-03: Channel choice

A client should be able to select available notification channels and change those preferences later.

## BR-04: Operational supportability

Operations and Client Services should be able to understand the lifecycle of an alert and investigate delivery questions without accessing more personal data than necessary.

## BR-05: Regulatory and trust posture

Notifications must remain informational. The capability must support applicable market-conduct, privacy, consent, and fair-access controls.

## BR-06: Measurable service

The product team should be able to measure adoption, alert processing, delivery outcomes, failures, and user-facing performance without exposing sensitive personal data in operational reporting.

## BR-07: Phased delivery

The first release should focus on a small set of understandable alert types and approved investor-channel delivery options. Additional event types and channels may be considered later.
