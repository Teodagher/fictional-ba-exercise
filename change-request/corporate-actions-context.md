# Corporate Actions Context

This fictional context describes the information domain involved in CR-01. It does not decide the solution.

## Event information

A corporate-action record may include:

- Issuer and instrument identifiers.
- Event type.
- Announcement or publication timestamp.
- Relevant dates, where present.
- Event status.
- A source reference.
- A version or update indicator, if provided by the source.
- Structured terms and explanatory text.

The available fields, update semantics, identifier quality, and publication guarantees require confirmation with the Corporate Actions team.

## Potential information sources

- Corporate Actions master data service.
- Investor Workspace event-details service.
- Existing document or announcement repository.
- Client Services knowledge base.

The authoritative source and integration contract have not been selected for this request.

## Operational considerations raised in discovery

- Some events may be corrected, withdrawn, or replaced.
- Event timing may differ across markets and event types.
- A source update may change client-relevant information without changing the event type.
- Historical records may be needed to explain what was shown or notified at a point in time.
- The full event details may contain terminology that requires controlled presentation and review.
