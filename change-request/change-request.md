# Change Request: Corporate Action Notification Events

**Request ID:** CR-01

**Requesting group:** Investor Services Product Management

**Status:** New request for analysis; not approved for delivery

## Request

Clients have asked to receive an informational notification when a corporate action associated with an instrument in their watchlist is published or materially updated. Product Management wants to assess whether a first release of corporate-action notifications could be added to the Market Monitor capability.

The requested notification should direct the client to the Investor Workspace for the full event information. It must not advise the client what action to take and must not support elections, instructions, order placement, or other transaction processing.

## Requested behavior to assess

- A client can opt in to corporate-action notifications for eligible instruments or watchlist items.
- A notification identifies the instrument and the corporate-action event type using approved source information.
- A notification links to the relevant event details in the Investor Workspace.
- A material update to an event may require a new notification, subject to an agreed repeat and update rule.
- The user can pause or remove this notification type using the existing preference journey.

## Business reason

Corporate-action information is time-sensitive and clients currently depend on manual checking or separate communications. Product believes that a controlled notification could increase the usefulness of watchlists and reduce avoidable Client Services questions.

## Questions for analysis

- Which existing requirements, features, services, data sources, and controls would be affected?
- What new behavior, data, interfaces, roles, or operational processes may be needed?
- Which stakeholders must review the request?
- What are the regulatory, privacy, security, accessibility, reliability, and support implications?
- Can the request fit the current release boundary, or should it be treated as a later phase?
- What information is missing before an estimate or delivery decision can be made?

## Constraints stated by the requester

- No transaction processing or client election workflow.
- No investment advice or recommendation language.
- Use approved Euronext information sources.
- Preserve user control over notification preferences.
- Maintain an auditable history of notifications and relevant changes.
