# Personas

Based on 12 discovery interviews (July 2026) and Workspace analytics. Share of MAU figures are fictional estimates.

## P1: Retail monitor — "Sophie", 58, Lyon

- **~55% of MAU.** Follows 3–8 instruments, mostly large French caps and one or two ETFs. Checks the Workspace several times a week, mainly on web.
- Wants simple alerts and clear wording. Limited knowledge of market-data terms; doesn't know if prices are delayed.
- Language: French. Mobile push: not enabled.
- Quote: *"Just tell me when it goes above €100. And tell me in French."*

## P2: Active private investor — "Luca", 34, Milan

- **~30% of MAU.** Follows 25+ instruments across Milan, Paris, and Amsterdam. Uses mobile daily, web at weekends.
- Wants control over thresholds, channels, pauses, and repeats. Very sensitive to duplicates and noise — "three notifications for the same move and I switch everything off".
- Language: Italian / English. Push enabled.

## P3: Professional market user — "Marta", 45, Lisbon

- **~9% of MAU**, but ~60% of real-time entitlements. Works at a small asset manager; monitors a broad list during office hours.
- Needs reliable timestamps with a time zone, and predictable behaviour at the open, auctions, and halts.
- Does not want the product to be confused with a trading or execution service.
- Language: English. Lives one hour behind CET.

## P4: Client Services analyst — "Niamh", Dublin support hub

- Handles ~40 tickets per day across EN/FR/NL. Level 1 support.
- Needs a small, auditable view of rule and delivery status, without unlimited access to personal data.
- Pain today: "I can't see anything, I escalate everything to IT and wait 4 days."

## P5: Product and operations owner — "Joost", Amsterdam

- Monitors adoption, service quality, failures, coverage, and operational risk.
- Needs aggregated dashboards (no personal data), a controlled coverage configuration process with four-eyes approval, and the ability to use the kill switch.
