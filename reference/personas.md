# Personas

## P1: Retail monitor

A private investor who follows a small number of instruments and checks the Investor Workspace several times a week. Wants simple alerts and clear wording. Has limited knowledge of market-data terminology.

## P2: Active private investor

A private investor who follows many instruments and uses mobile and web channels. Wants control over thresholds, channels, pauses, and repeated events. Is sensitive to duplicate notifications.

## P3: Professional market user

A professional user who monitors a broader set of instruments during working hours. Needs reliable timestamps and predictable behavior. Does not want the product to be confused with a trading or execution service.

## P4: Client Services analyst

An internal support user who investigates client questions. Needs a small, auditable view of rule and delivery status without unrestricted access to personal data.

## P5: Product and operations owner

An internal user who monitors adoption, service quality, failures, coverage configuration, and operational risk. Needs aggregated measures and controlled configuration workflows.
