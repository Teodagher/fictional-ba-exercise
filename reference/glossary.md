# Glossary

**Alert rule:** A saved user configuration that defines an instrument, condition, and one or more notification channels.

**Alert event:** An eligible market event that matches an active alert rule.

**Channel provider:** An internal or external service used to hand off a notification for delivery.

**Correlation identifier:** A non-user-facing identifier used to connect processing and delivery records across services.

**Eligible instrument:** An instrument included in the approved coverage configuration.

**Investor Workspace:** The fictional Euronext client-facing web and mobile experience used in this case study.

**Notification attempt:** A recorded attempt to hand a notification to a selected channel provider.

**Notification history:** The in-app record of notifications relevant to a user.

**Rule lifecycle:** The states and transitions of an alert rule, including creation, active, paused, resumed, and deleted.

**Unusual volume:** A volume condition whose business definition and calculation method must be agreed for the product.
