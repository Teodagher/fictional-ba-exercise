# Glossary

**Alert rule:** A saved user configuration: an instrument, a condition, and one or more notification channels.

**Alert event:** An eligible market event that matches an active alert rule.

**Auction (opening / closing):** A trading phase where orders are collected and matched at a single price. On Euronext cash markets, the pre-opening phase runs 07:15–09:00 CET and the closing auction from 17:30 CET.

**Channel provider:** An internal or external service used to hand a notification over for delivery (email provider, mobile push provider).

**Continuous trading:** The main trading phase (09:00–17:30 CET) where orders are matched continuously.

**Corporate action (CA):** An event decided by an issuer that affects its securities, for example a dividend, split, or rights issue.

**Correlation ID:** An identifier, not shown to the user, used to connect processing and delivery records across services.

**Delayed data:** Market data shown with a 15-minute delay. Under MiFID II, this is available free of charge to the public.

**DORA:** Digital Operational Resilience Act (EU 2022/2554). Covers ICT risk, incident reporting, and ICT third-party providers.

**DPIA:** Data Protection Impact Assessment, required under GDPR for high-risk processing.

**EAA:** European Accessibility Act. Applies to many digital services since 28 June 2025. Technical reference: EN 301 549 / WCAG 2.1 AA.

**Eligible instrument:** An instrument included in the approved coverage configuration.

**Entitlement:** A user's right, under a data licence, to see real-time data.

**ePrivacy:** The EU ePrivacy Directive. Governs consent for electronic marketing messages (email, push).

**Ex-date:** The first day on which a share trades without the right to the upcoming dividend or corporate action.

**GDPR:** General Data Protection Regulation (EU 2016/679).

**Investor Workspace:** The fictional Euronext client-facing web and mobile experience used in this case study.

**ISIN:** International Securities Identification Number (ISO 6166). A 12-character code identifying a security, e.g. NL0000000001 (fictional).

**Kill switch:** An operational control to stop all notifications, for one instrument or globally, in an emergency.

**MAR:** Market Abuse Regulation (EU 596/2014). Covers insider dealing, market manipulation, and investment recommendations.

**MIC:** Market Identifier Code (ISO 10383). Identifies a trading venue, e.g. XPAR (Paris), XAMS (Amsterdam), XBRU (Brussels), XLIS (Lisbon), XMSM (Dublin), MTAA (Milan), XOSL (Oslo).

**MiFID II:** Markets in Financial Instruments Directive II (EU 2014/65) and MiFIR. Covers record-keeping, market data, and investor protection.

**Notification attempt:** A recorded attempt to hand a notification to a channel provider.

**Notification history:** The in-app record of notifications relevant to a user.

**Real-time data:** Market data shown without delay. Available only to users with an entitlement.

**Record date:** The date on which a shareholder must be on the register to receive the corporate-action benefit.

**Reservation / trading halt:** A temporary interruption of trading on an instrument, for example when the price moves outside its allowed range.

**Rule lifecycle:** The states and transitions of an alert rule: created, active, paused, resumed, triggered, expired, deleted.

**Suspension:** A decision to stop trading on an instrument, usually for longer and for regulatory reasons.

**Unusual volume:** A volume condition whose business definition and calculation method are still to be agreed.
